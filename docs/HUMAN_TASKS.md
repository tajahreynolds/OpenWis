# Human-Only Task Registry


This file is the canonical list of tasks that agents must never perform.
Only humans may edit this file.


## Why This Exists


Agents are good at code, docs, and data. Humans are required for trust,
relationships, ethics, and community. Confusing the two causes harm.


## Categories


### Community & Relational
- Recruiting participants
- Communicating with participants
- Facilitating deliberation sessions
- Building trust with community groups
- Handling grievances or conflict
- Deciding when to pause or stop engagement


### Institutional
- Contacting city councils, NGOs, or governments
- Negotiating partnerships
- Making commitments on behalf of the project
- Signing agreements or MOUs


### Ethical & Judgment
- Deciding what questions to ask communities
- Interpreting feedback with cultural context
- Deciding what data is appropriate to collect
- Assessing whether participation is extractive
- Deciding how to compensate participants


### Communications
- Public announcements
- Press releases
- Social media posts
- Blog posts (community-facing)
- Fundraising appeals


### Financial & Legal
- Managing budgets
- Paying participants or facilitators
- Signing contracts
- Legal compliance decisions
- Donor relations


### Personnel
- Hiring
- Firing
- Performance reviews
- Volunteer coordination


## What To Do If You Encounter a Human Task


1. Stop immediately.
2. Create an issue with label `human-required`.
3. Describe the task and why it requires a human.
4. Do not attempt a partial or automated version.
5. Move to a different agent-eligible task.
